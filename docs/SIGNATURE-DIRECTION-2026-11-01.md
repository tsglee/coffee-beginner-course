# Signature redesign direction

The previous five homepage treatments retain the same long sequence, similar typography/photo combinations and too little meaningful interaction. The next page should earn attention through one memorable composition and let coffee-specific interaction carry the experience.

Creative authority is delegated in the user's earlier words, 全部交給你去設計你就是網頁設計師. Product questions and the request to decide visual details are already answered in this conversation, so this run uses the confirmed brief rather than repeating a concept approval gate.

## Seven grounded visual systems
1. A personal coffee journal: the visitor completes a flavor entry; blue paper, strong serif headings, photograph as the center of a spread.
2. A coffee tasting sheet: preferences act as the table of contents and lead into actual bean examples; structured light/dark typography and measurable interaction.
3. A Taiwanese neighborhood coffee noticeboard: personal teacher photographs and large direct handwritten-like quotations, without simulated handwritten decorations.
4. A small independent photography book: bold image sequencing, quiet margins and a tactile sky-blue cover; coffee remains the content rather than UI chrome.
5. A letter to a beginner: an inviting opening line and one central brewing photograph, with a flowing narrative from curiosity to practice.
6. A cafe window poster: a large brand-colored statement, concise course facts and a photographic window, with restrained supporting pages.
7. A roaster's monthly menu: an open menu of flavors and teacher-guided pairing, facts pulled from the current catalog, the visitor's choice changes the featured coffee.

Predictable category defaults rejected: a left headline plus right photograph followed by equal cards; its obvious opposite, a dark luxury hero with gold accents and the same cards. The brand is not being renamed and the supplied logo remains authoritative.

First viewport: horizontal brand navigation; dominant one-line Chinese title; wide editorial hand-pouring photograph, with a functional lower-corner course/bean inquiry plate. The memorable event is the photograph/title spread and its generous offset frame. Below, a large real teacher photograph and dense course facts precede an interactive bean spread. The selection changes the featured image, coffee and notes while keeping the direct LINE action in place. Keyboard and native buttons work throughout.

Success: a new visitor can identify the beginner workshop and roasted beans in seconds, understand the real people behind the class, explore a flavor and open LINE without a cart. Risk: the opening is deliberately image-led; the inquiry plate must remain legible and near the first screen on phones.
