## Brand & Style

This design system channels an artisanal, high-end editorial publication reminiscent of high-grade Japanese and Taiwanese lifestyle print journals (e.g., *Kinfolk*, *CASA Brutus*, *Fountain*). Designed specifically for a discerning Taiwanese audience seeking sensory mastery, mindful craft, and personal enrichment, the aesthetic avoids corporate tech tropes in favor of tactile calm, deliberate precision, and poetic cadence.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Asymmetry & Tension:** Unbalanced, deliberate two-column or magazine-style spreads rather than modular card grids. Images bleed intentionally or sit framed in disciplined hair-thin margins.
- **Micro-Texture & Precision:** Line weights never exceed 1px hairline rules. Brass accents perform structural and notation roles (e.g., chapter tags, water flow rates, coordinate marks).
- **Sensory Atmosphere:** Generous negative space acts as a breathing pause—mirroring the stillness of the coffee bloom (悶蒸) and the slow draw-down of a manual brew.
- **Dual-Language Harmony:** Chinese Songti/Serif displays paired comfortably alongside Western humanist and geometric typography, respecting proportional rhythm between Han characters and Latin glyphs.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the physical elements of third-wave pour-over ritual: oxidized kettle metal, patinated brass scales, unbleached cotton dripper papers, and high-density slow-roasted beans.

### Color Roles & Surface Values
- **Primary (`#103E46` - Midnight Teal):** Anchors high-contrast typography, primary buttons, inverted editorial sections (curated night brewing modules), and structural rule lines.
- **Secondary (`#BC9563` - Muted Brass):** Reserved for delicate metallic accents, chapter indicators, bespoke hairline dividers, and precision metrics (ratio, temperature, extraction time).
- **Tertiary (`#1A2123` - Deep Espresso Charcoal):** Primary reading color for long-form narrative typography, high-legibility subheaders, and micro-labels.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F4F0E8` - Ivory):** The predominant background surface, offering warm paper texture and softening the glare of pure white.
- **Neutral Soft (`#FAF8F4` - Subtle Cream):** Alternating background tiers, image frame mats, and inset commentary blocks.
- **Hairline Border (`rgba(16, 62, 70, 0.12)`): Ultra-light teal line applied to structural borders, frame edges, and metadata dividers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy embraces traditional literary publishing standards, using an upright serif for Taiwanese Mandarin (`Noto Serif`) alongside monospaced technical notations (`Space Mono`) to reference laboratory brew logs.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Section Titles:** Set in `Noto Serif` with ample vertical line-height (1.3–1.5) to give multi-line Chinese poetry and statements room to resonate.
- **Body & Editorial Reading:** Rendered in `Noto Sans` with relaxed line heights (`28px` on `15px` size) and gentle kerning to reduce visual fatigue across long paragraph blocks.
- **Technical & Metric Metadata:** Brew parameters (water temperature `92°C`, ratio `1:15`, bloom time `00:45s`, chapter indexes `VOL. 01`) must always utilize `Space Mono` in uppercase styling with extended letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an asymmetrical, magazine-style 12-column grid system rather than repetitive stacked grids.

### Responsive Behavior & Grid Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with generous `4rem` outer canvas padding and `2rem` gutters. Content alternates between wide spans (e.g., 7 columns text, 5 columns framed imagery) and centered intimate columns (6-column centered essays).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Images stack below narrative leads or align in an offset 5:3 arrangement.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.5rem` outer margin and `1rem` gutters. Editorial rhythm transitions to full-width cards framed with hairlines, separated by generous vertical padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

### Whitespace Philosophy
Vertical spacing creates dramatic visual pauses. Chapter intros receive `space-xl` (80px+) above and below them. Margin dividers are delicate 1px lines inset from the page boundaries rather than full bleed edges.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an artisanal, tactile print aesthetic, this system completely rejects generic drop shadows, diffused box shadows, and 3D skeuomorphism. Depth is achieved purely through **tonal layers**, **optical matting**, and **overlapping planes**.

### Depth Layers
- **Base Canvas:** Solid Ivory `#F4F0E8`.
- **Level 1 (Framed Containers & Photo Mats):** Background `#FAF8F4` bounded by a 1px solid hairline `rgba(16, 62, 70, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Inverted Anchor Zones):** Full-bleed Midnight Teal `#103E46` sections with Ivory and Brass text, suggesting deep evening coffee study.
- **Overlapping Framing:** Photography utilizes double-line mat borders (an outer 1px teal boundary with an inner 8px white matte border and a micro brass corner accent) to create dimension through traditional framing craftsmanship.
- **Interactive Planes (Flyouts, Select Modals):** Crisp 1px solid Midnight Teal border `#103E46` on a `#FAF8F4` surface with zero blur shadow, offset by 4px solid brass shadow block if physical elevation is required.

## Shapes

The design system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`), referencing vintage print blocks, archival bookbinding, copper dripper stands, and trimmed unbleached paper.

### Form Guidance
- All photo containers, interactive buttons, modal frames, and input fields utilize precise `0px` border radiuses.
- Decorative accent tags and numbered badges feature crisp rectangular bounds or fine rectangular outlines.
- Circular geometry is restricted exclusively to literal mechanical symbols: radial coffee flavor wheels, clock extract timers, and compass marks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Dark):** Solid `#103E46` Midnight Teal background, `#F4F0E8` Ivory text, 0px radius, 1px `#103E46` border. Padding `14px 32px`. Transitions on hover to `#1A2123` with a subtle `#BC9563` interior border stroke.
- **Secondary Action (Brass Frame):** Transparent background, 1px solid `#BC9563` hairline outline, `#103E46` text. Hover fills background with `#BC9563` and flips text to `#FAF8F4`.
- **Text Link Action:** Understated text in `#103E46` or `#BC9563` with an active 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline; expands letter spacing on hover.

### Numbered Chapter Badges
- Chapter indicators (e.g., `CHAPTER 01 / 認識產區與生豆`) sit inside an inline-block rectangular hairline badge.
- Outer border: 1px `#BC9563` with a left-accent fill block for the numeral. Font set to `label-chapter` in `#BC9563`.

### Photo Frames & Editorial Mats
- Photographic components feature a museum mount treatment: image is placed inside a container with `12px` to `20px` padding of `#FAF8F4`, enclosed in a 1px hairline border (`#103E46` at 15% opacity), accompanied by an optional top-right brass crosshair (`+`) marker.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- Background: `#FAF8F4`.
- Border: 1px bottom-line only or full 1px boundary in `#103E46` (40% opacity). Focus state deepens to 1px solid `#103E46` with an accompanying subtle brass label caption.
- Typography: `body-md` in `#1A2123`, placeholder in `#103E46` (40% opacity).

### Flavor & Technique Chips
- Pill shapes are prohibited. Chips are sharp, slim rectangular tags with 1px border `#BC9563` or muted teal tone, padding `4px 10px`, with text set in `label-brass`.

### Course Curriculum Accordion & Lists
- Divided by 1px horizontal hairline rules in `#103E46` at 15% opacity.
- Left column: Time and Step Number in `Space Mono` (`#BC9563`).
- Right column: Lesson Title in `Noto Serif` (`#103E46`), expandable body copy in `Noto Sans` (`#1A2123`).
